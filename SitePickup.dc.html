How far ahead should I order?

Three days for everyday bakes. For custom cakes and cookies, please give at least two weeks. Spring and the holidays fill up faster.

How do I pay?

After I confirm your order, I'll email you an invoice you can pay by card. Custom orders need a 50% deposit to hold the date.

Can you work around allergies?

I can leave out nuts or make some recipes dairy-free, but my kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts every day. I can't promise anything is allergen-free.

What if I need to change or cancel?

Changes are fine up to three days before your date. Deposits can move to a new date once, but they aren't refundable.

Can I send a picture for my cake?

Please do. Reply to your confirmation email with photos or a Pinterest board, and I'll make it my own.

Do you ship?

Not yet. Everything is baked for pickup or delivery around Tulsa, so it arrives the way it should.
